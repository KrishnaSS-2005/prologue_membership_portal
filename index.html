ABOUT PROLOGUE

More than a reading club.

PROLOGUE is a space where books become conversations, ideas become connections, and readers become a community.

01

Our Vision

To create a vibrant reading culture where students discover new perspectives, share ideas and develop a lifelong relationship with literature.

02

Our Purpose

PROLOGUE encourages students to read, discuss, create and connect through books, stories and meaningful literary experiences.

03

Our Community

Whether you read every day or are simply curious about books, PROLOGUE welcomes students with different interests and perspectives.
